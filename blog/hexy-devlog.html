Building a Custom AI Engine for Mobile: The Story Behind HEX:RESISTANCE

GET IT ON GOOGLE PLAY

I'm thrilled to announce that HEX:RESISTANCE is now officially live on the Google Play Store! Building this game has been a masterclass in modern AI engineering, allowing me to merge classical search algorithms with cutting-edge LLM development workflows.

Engineering the AI Opponent

Standard state-machine logic wasn't going to cut it for a hex-based strategy game; the state space is simply too massive and deterministic patterns become obvious. To provide a deeply challenging and dynamic opponent, I engineered a custom Minimax algorithm equipped with Alpha-Beta pruning.

Deploying computationally expensive search trees on mobile devices is notoriously difficult. Deep recursive evaluations rapidly drain battery life, block the main UI thread, and trigger native Out-Of-Memory (OOM) crashes due to excessive object allocation during the search.

Accelerated by Generative AI

To overcome these severe hardware constraints quickly, I heavily leveraged generative AI tools. Acting as the architect, I utilized Google's Antigravity (Claude) as my UI/UX designer and Codex (OpenAI) as my engineering co-pilot. This allowed me to rapidly refactor the engine's state representation—eliminating object allocation entirely with in-place board mutations—and offload the intensive computation safely.

This hybrid approach to AI engineering allowed me to completely build, optimize, and ship the game in just about two months.
